Anti-Proxy System Troubleshooting

1. Face Encoding Errors: Ensure face photos uploaded during registration are well-lit with clear visibility of eyes and nose.

2. CCTV Video Verification: Upload videos in `.mp4` format. Ensure camera resolution is at least 720p for optimal facial detection accuracy.

3. Status Flags: If status reads NOT_DETECTED, check if the student is enrolled in the facial database or upload a clearer baseline profile photo.
